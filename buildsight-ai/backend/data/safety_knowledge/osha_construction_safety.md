# Construction Site Safety Manual — PPE Requirements & Regulations

## OSHA 29 CFR 1926 — Construction Safety Standards

### Hard Hat Requirements
- All workers on construction sites must wear a hard hat (safety helmet) at all times.
- Class E (Electrical) helmets required when working near electrical hazards.
- Helmets must comply with ANSI/ISEA Z89.1 standards.
- Replace helmets after any significant impact, even if no visible damage.

### Safety Vest Requirements
- High-visibility (hi-vis) safety vests required in all active work areas.
- Class 2 vest minimum for roadway construction.
- Class 3 vest required for workers in areas with motorized traffic.
- Vests must comply with ANSI/ISEA 107 standards.

### Danger Zone Protocols
- Restricted zones must be marked with physical barriers and warning signage.
- No unauthorized personnel permitted in hazardous areas.
- All workers entering danger zones must be briefed on specific hazards.
- A safety officer must be present during operations in high-risk zones.

### Height Work Safety (Working at Height)
- Fall protection required for work at heights of 6 feet or more.
- Full-body harness and lanyard required for elevated work.
- Safety nets or fall arrest systems must be installed where feasible.
- Daily inspection of fall protection equipment is mandatory.

### Scaffolding Regulations
- Scaffolding must be erected, dismantled, and altered by trained scaffolders.
- Minimum platform width: 18 inches (450mm).
- Guard rails required on platforms more than 10 feet above lower levels.
- Scaffolding must be capable of supporting 4x the intended load.

### Heavy Machinery Safety
- Exclusion zones required around all operating heavy equipment.
- Workers near heavy machinery must wear high-visibility vests.
- Spotters required when visibility is limited.
- All machinery operators must hold valid certifications.

### Fire Safety on Construction Sites
- Fire extinguishers must be available within 75 feet of any work area.
- No smoking within 25 feet of flammable materials.
- Hot work permits required for welding, cutting, or grinding.
- Emergency evacuation routes must be clearly marked and unobstructed.

### Electrical Safety
- All electrical work must be performed by licensed electricians.
- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures required before electrical maintenance.
- Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter (GFCI) required on all outdoor circuits.
- Maintain minimum 10-foot clearance from energized overhead power lines.

### Chemical Hazard Protocols
- Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) must be available for all chemicals.
- Appropriate PPE must be worn when handling hazardous materials.
- Spill containment kits must be available in chemical storage areas.
- Proper labeling and storage of all hazardous materials is mandatory.

### Emergency Response
- Emergency contact numbers must be posted at site entrance.
- First aid kit must be accessible at all times on site.
- Nearest hospital and emergency services must be identified before work begins.
- All incidents must be reported to the site supervisor immediately.
